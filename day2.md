Day 2: What Is Prompt Engineering

🚀 ABTalks 60 Days Claude Challenge – Day X

Prompt Engineering is the skill of communicating effectively with AI.

The difference between a weak prompt and a well-engineered prompt can be the difference between a generic answer and a highly valuable result.

💡 Key takeaway:
The quality of AI output depends heavily on the quality of your input.

✅ Be specific
✅ Provide context
✅ Define the desired format
✅ Include constraints when needed

Better prompts → Better AI responses → Better productivity
